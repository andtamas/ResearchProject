# Digital self-control: feature separation and high-effort limits

Experimental part
- Data used: scenario-based survey data with three variables (app blocker type, bypass likelihood on a 1-5 scale, and perceived effort on a 1-5 scale).
- Experiment design: an A/B test using a distraction scenario. Group A evaluates the standard 15-minute timer. Group B evaluates the PC-unlock barrier.
- Validation method: a t-test comparing the mean scores between both groups.
- Advantages: it shifts the burden from psychological willpower to physical hardware friction.

Realistic validation planning
- Data collection: a survey distributed to computer science students.
- Sample size: a minimum of 30 valid responses.
- Data cleaning: the script will remove incomplete responses and those completed in under 30 seconds.
